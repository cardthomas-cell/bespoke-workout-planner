Privacy Policy – Bespoke Workout Planner

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Bespoke Workout Planner ("the app") is made by Thomas Card. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information you enter

You can enter workout names, exercise names, weights, sets, reps, timer durations and notes. This information is saved only in the app's local storage on your device so that your workouts are there next time you open the app. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

No accounts, analytics or advertising

  • The app has no sign-up or login.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking, crash-reporting or advertising software.
  • The app does not connect to the internet to send or receive your data.
  • The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Device permissions

The app does not ask for access to your location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone, health data or notifications. It uses your device's vibration (haptics) when a timer finishes. This doesn't involve any personal data.

Apple

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide crash reports and usage statistics to us in anonymised form. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your workouts stay on your device until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup, if you have it turned on) may include the app's data, and Apple handles those backups under its own terms.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
