Bespoke Workout Planner

Build workouts from warm-ups, strength sets, supersets, HIIT rounds, stretches and cool-downs, then run them with built-in rest and interval timers. No account needed, and your workouts stay on your device.

Support

Questions, problems or feature requests? Email card-thomas-ncvd3n@mail.grokbot.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

Common questions

Where are my workouts saved? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your workouts.

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up or login.

How do I start a workout? On the home screen, tap Start next to a plan. Use "Skip section", "Skip rest" and "Rest now" to control the session.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. In short, the app collects no data.
